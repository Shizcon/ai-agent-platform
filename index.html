Three ways AI goes to work for you

Pick the job you want off your plate. Each service starts as an agent you build here — trained on your business, tested in a live chat, and live in minutes.

Every customer gets an answer — straight away, in the language they wrote in. The agent handles questions, orders and bookings on your website, WhatsApp and phone line, day and night.

  • Replies in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu — matching the customer
  • Takes orders and books appointments while you sleep
  • No message left waiting — not even at 3 in the morning

An extra pair of hands for your team. The agent drafts replies, sums up long threads, prepares the daily report and chases follow-ups — the small jobs that quietly eat the whole day.

  • Drafts emails, messages and documents in your tone
  • Summarises meetings, chats and long files in seconds
  • Keeps task lists, reminders and follow-ups moving

The same steps, done the same way, every single time. Turn the routine your team repeats daily — collecting details, status updates, payment reminders — into an agent that simply gets on with it.

  • Turns repeated steps into one clear instruction list
  • Collects details step by step and confirms before finishing
  • Fewer mistakes, and no follow-up ever forgotten
